How a clip is made

H3 does not write a video the way a chatbot writes a sentence. It starts from random noise and slowly turns that noise into 24 pictures per second and stereo sound, together, in one network.

01

The product in one line

A server that is already running. An agent that only fills in the prompt.

A long-lived ComfyUI on a DGX Spark holds the models in memory. You SSH in, ask Cursor or Claude, and get an mp4 (24 fps video + 32 kHz stereo). One job at a time.

02

Not a chatbot

Everyday LLMH3 (this project)
Writes one word at a timeCleans the whole video at once, several times
You see words appearUntil the last pass, you only have noisy sludge
Remembers past wordsRemembers nothing between passes
Cost ≈ number of wordsCost ≈ steps × picture size × length

At 50% of the work you do not have half a video. You have a full-length clip that still looks like noise.

03

Five stages of one job

Think of a film lab, not a chat box.

  1. Read the request. Qwen3-VL-32B turns your words into numbers. It does not write the video. Default Ref2VA also reads 1–9 identity stills (--ref-image).
  2. Shrink. Two small networks (VAEs) compress pictures and sound so the big model can chew them. Attached stills are compressed, not invented.
  3. Pack. Text, optional identity stills, video noise, and audio noise sit in one list. Conditioning is other rows in that list, not a side door.
  4. Clean the noise. Default Ref2VA does this 4 times (official Ref2V Turbo). Selectable FL2VA does it 8 times. Video and sound use two different clocks — that is why ComfyUI must be new enough for ModelSamplingAV.
  5. Grow back. VAEs expand to frames and stereo. SPAN doubles 960×544 to 1920×1088. Crop 8 rows to 1080p. Write the mp4.
