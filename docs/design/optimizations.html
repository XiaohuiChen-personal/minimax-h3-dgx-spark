Go faster without breaking sound

Turn these on in order. Do not enable every node at maximum. That still fits in memory and can still ruin the audio.

02

Never turn on

These look like free speed. They are not, on H3.
  • EasyCache — two clocks; it muffles audio. Do not pair it with FirstBlockCache.
  • SageAttention 3 — broken on GB10.
  • --use-sage-attention as a launch flag — blind; H3 noise reports.
  • Sol-Attn flex_attention — wrong numbers on this chip (cosine ≈ 0.92).
  • Turbo-SLA plus Sol-Attn — two sparsities. Pick Sol-Attn.
  • --lowvram — slow copy on unified memory; no extra room.
03

Why “all at once” can still fail

  • Two clocks. A cache that only watches video will lie to the audio track.
  • Pruned AdaLN. The 8-step Comfy-Org LoRA fits the pruned DiT. The 4-step pack does not, for dialogue.
  • Sage cliff. Some kernels emit noise above ~160k tokens. Native 1080p / 15 s is over that. 960×544 / 8 s is ~25k.
  • Cache vs Turbo. At 8 distilled steps, FirstBlockCache may skip almost nothing. Keep H3 Safe. Do not promise a 4× cache win.
